What Is Remote Monitoring for Chronic Care?

Remote monitoring supports ongoing chronic care by giving care teams visibility into patients’ health between consultations. Regular home readings help teams review trends, identify changes that may need attention and coordinate follow-up. SmartFuture brings readings, trends and alerts into one platform to support ongoing care.

Common Questions

What is remote monitoring for chronic care?

Remote monitoring allows patients to take health readings at home and share them with their care team between consultations. SmartFuture brings readings, trends and alerts into one platform to support ongoing chronic care.

Does the patient need a smartphone?

Not necessarily. SmartFuture supports different monitoring models depending on the programme and devices, including Bluetooth-connected apps or tablets and cellular-enabled devices.

What if the patient has no internet at home?

Depending on the programme, cellular-enabled medical devices can transmit supported readings over the mobile network without requiring the patient’s home Wi-Fi.

How do connected medical devices send readings?

This depends on the device and programme. Supported devices can transmit readings through Bluetooth-connected apps or tablets, cellular connectivity or other supported integrations.

How is this different from your Hospital at Home solution?

Hospital at Home supports more intensive, hospital-led care in patients’ homes. Chronic Care focuses on ongoing monitoring and follow-up for long-term conditions between consultations.

Which conditions can it be used for?

The platform can support monitoring programmes for conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, cardiovascular conditions and other long-term conditions, with devices and workflows configured around the care programme.

Does it connect to our EMR?

SmartFuture supports APIs and integrations with existing clinical systems, depending on your systems and integration requirements.

Can we run it under our own brand?

Yes. SmartFuture supports white-label deployments, allowing the platform and patient-facing interfaces to be configured around your organisation’s brand.
