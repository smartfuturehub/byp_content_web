Common Questions

What is facility-based patient monitoring?

Facility-based patient monitoring connects residents’ health readings, devices, alerts and care information in one platform, giving care teams a shared view across a nursing home, senior living or residential care facility.

Do residents have to do anything?

It depends on the monitoring device and workflow. Supported wearable and connected devices can capture readings with minimal resident interaction and send them to the platform automatically.

Does it work everywhere in the building?

Connectivity and coverage are planned around each facility’s layout, monitoring requirements and the areas where residents receive care.

What happens if a resident leaves the building?

Where exit or movement monitoring is configured, supported devices can trigger an alert when a resident moves beyond a designated area.

How is this different from your home monitoring solutions?

Home monitoring follows one patient in their own house. This is built for a building: many residents at once, staff on shift, coverage across every room, and one screen for the floor.

Does it connect to the systems we already use?

Yes. We integrate with facility and clinical systems so readings land in the resident record, working to your IT team's requirements.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size and layout of the building. Most facilities start with one floor or one wing, prove it works, then extend.

Can we run it under our own brand?

Yes. SmartFuture is white-label, so the dashboard and the reports can carry your organisation's name and look.
