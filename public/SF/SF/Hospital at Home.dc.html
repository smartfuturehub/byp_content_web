What Is Hospital at Home?

Hospital at Home enables patients to receive hospital-led care in their own homes instead of staying in a hospital bed. Care teams can remotely monitor vital signs, communicate with patients and coordinate care while maintaining visibility of the patient’s progress.

SmartFuture provides the technology that connects the patient, medical devices and care team — bringing readings, alerts, communication and workflows into one platform.

How It Works

From patient setup to monitoring, alerts and follow-up, SmartFuture brings your hospital-at-home workflow into one platform.

1

Set Up the Patient

For connected monitoring, patients receive a tablet and the medical devices required for their care programme, configured and paired before use. Patients using their own devices can submit readings through a simple web link.

2

Monitor From the Dashboard

Connected devices can upload readings automatically, while manual and photo-based readings appear in the same dashboard. Your care team can review vital signs, trends and patient progress in one place.

3

Review Alerts and Follow Up

Readings outside clinician-configured limits can trigger alerts, helping your care team identify readings that need review and coordinate follow-up with the patient.

Common Questions

What is hospital at home?

It is a hospital programme where the patient is treated and monitored in their own home instead of on a ward. The hospital stays responsible for the care. Technology like SmartFuture's provides the devices, the monitoring and the link back to the care team.

How are patients monitored safely at home?

Clinical teams decide which patients are suitable for hospital-at-home care and configure monitoring requirements and alert thresholds. SmartFuture helps the care team monitor readings, identify readings that require review and coordinate follow-up.

What does the patient need at home?

It depends on the care programme. Patients who need connected monitoring can receive a tablet and paired medical devices. Other patients can use their own devices and submit readings through a simple web link.

What if the patient is not good with technology?

The tablet shows one simple screen and stays plugged in. For most patients the only step is taking the reading — the device sends it by itself. Caregivers and nurses can reach the patient by video from the same screen.

Does it connect to our EMR or EHR?

Yes. SmartFuture can integrate with EMR/EHR and other hospital systems through supported APIs and integrations. We work with your IT team to align the integration with your workflows, infrastructure and security requirements.

Can we run it under our own brand?

Yes. SmartFuture is white-label, so the platform and the patient app can carry your hospital's name and look.

Which conditions can it be used for?

It is not limited to one condition. Care plans are configured per patient group, so the same platform supports post-surgical recovery, heart and lung conditions, diabetes, maternity care and general recovery after a hospital stay.

How do we start?

Most teams start with one ward or one patient group, prove it works, then grow. Tell us who you want to care for at home and we will scope it with you.

Does every patient need a kit?

No. Patients who need connected monitoring can use a tablet with paired medical devices. Other patients can use their own devices and submit readings through a simple web link, including links shared through WhatsApp. Both appear on the same care team dashboard.
